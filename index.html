Welcome

Welcome to Khando Tibetan Kitchen & Guest House

Nestled along Boudha Road, Khando Tibetan Kitchen & Guest House offers a peaceful sanctuary from the bustling streets of Kathmandu. Whether you are stopping by for a hearty meal or looking for a cozy stay, our women-owned establishment welcomes you with open doors and sincere warmth. Enjoy authentic Tibetan recipes passed down through generations, served against the iconic backdrop of the Boudhanath Stupa.

About Us

Our Story & Vision

Where tradition, culture, and community meet in the heart of Boudha.

Our Journey

Located in the heart of Boudha, Kathmandu, Khando Tibetan Kitchen & Guest House was created to offer a place where tradition, culture, and community meet. As a proud women-owned enterprise, our focus has always been on genuine care, authentic flavors, and preserving traditional culinary techniques.

Freshness & Quality

Every dish is prepared fresh upon order using hand-selected ingredients.

Community & Peace

We strive to create a serene space where guests can unwind, catch up with friends, or reflect in quiet contemplation.

Cultural Authenticity

From our decor to our kitchen recipes, we proudly share the rich heritage of Tibetan food and hospitality.
